Online Fraud & Financial Cybercrime

verified_user Updated: March 2025 • Aligned with BNS & Information Technology Act
timer
Crucial • The Golden Hour 2 - 3 Hrs

Reporting financial fraud within 2 to 3 hours gives banks the legal mandate to freeze stolen funds before the fraudster cashes out.

What does this mean?

You do not need legal jargon to understand that taking your money deceitfully is a serious, cognizable offense under the Information Technology Act and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).

Unauthorized UPI / QR Scams Scanning a QR code never deposits money into your bank—it exclusively debits funds from your account.

What can you do?

Procedural Action
1
Contact Bank / Payment Provider Immediate

Request an immediate block on UPI and debit cards. Demand a formal Dispute Reference Number and 12-digit transaction UTR.

2
Call Helpline 1930 National

Keep transaction SMS handy. The operator initiates a real-time auto-freeze notification across beneficiary banking gateways.

3
File Formal Portal Complaint < 24 Hours

Log on to cybercrime.gov.in under ‘Report Cyber Crime > Financial Fraud’. Download the acknowledgment receipt (Acknowledgement Number).

4
Police Station & Zero-FIR Legal Stage

If total financial loss exceeds your state’s cyber threshold, visit the Cyber Cell. Officers are mandated to register a Zero-FIR regardless of territorial jurisdiction.

Important Safety Rule

Never share OTPs, ATM PINs, or install remote access software (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk) at the request of anyone claiming to be a police officer or bank fraud investigator. Real authorities never ask for screen access.
