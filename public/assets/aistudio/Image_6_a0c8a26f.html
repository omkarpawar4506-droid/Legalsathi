What does this mean?

Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, you are legally protected against unfair trade practices and hazardous goods. You possess 6 sovereign consumer rights:

health_and_safety 1. Safety

Protection from hazardous products & services.

info 2. Information

Accurate details on purity, price & ingredients.

interests 3. Choice

Assured access to competitive variety & pricing.

record_voice_over 4. Heard

Guaranteed voice in forums & dispute cells.

handshake 5. Redressal

Right to compensation, repairs, or full refund.

school 6. Education

Lifelong access to awareness & civic knowledge.

Action Hierarchy

4 Guided Steps
1

Direct Written Notice

Step 1

Send an email directly to the seller or brand’s grievance officer. Clearly cite the defective order ID, describe the deficiency, and demand resolution within 7-14 days.

lightbulb Always preserve automated ticket numbers & timestamps.
2

National Consumer Helpline

Pre-Litigation

Dial toll-free 1915 or submit via web portal. NCH facilitates direct mediation with registered corporate convergence partners without court proceedings.

consumerhelpline.gov.in open_in_new
3

e-Daakhil Online Filing

Govt Portal

File an official petition from your phone through the official e-Daakhil portal. No advocate is required for claims under ₹5 Lakhs, and nominal digital fees apply.

4

District Commission Hearing

Statutory Forum

If informal mediation stalls, the District Commission adjudicates binding financial compensations, order refunds, punitive damages, and legal costs.

Important Civic Distinction

LegalSaathi empowers you with statutory rights awareness and procedural guidance. LegalSaathi is not a government portal. All legally enforceable complaints must be filed on the official government systems (1915 NCH or e-Daakhil).
