## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Civic Tech Minimalism** with an **Empowering & Humanist Tone**, engineered specifically for citizens navigating complex legal, civil, and personal safety systems. The aesthetic strips away bureaucratic opacity, replacing intimidating legalese with warm, structural reassurance, lucid visual hierarchy, and immediate actionable clarity.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Everyday citizens, college students, working women, grassroots advocates, and non-lawyers seeking rapid legal first aid, rights awareness, and statutory safety workflows across India.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Unshakable constitutional trust, psychological safety, zero judgment, and active empowerment. The interface must feel as dependable as an established civic institution, yet as warm, patient, and conversational as a trusted companion.

### Design Principles
1. **Dignified Accessibility First:** Every color pair, text node, and icon must satisfy strict WCAG 2.1 AAA readability standards. Dense statutes are rendered into bite-sized, digestible modular components.
2. **Reassurance Through Structure:** Visual weight is grounded in stable geometry, deep indigos, and soft cream canvases that prevent sensory distress during high-stakes safety emergencies.
3. **Optimistic Guidance:** Safety actions and rights awareness use warm marigold and emerald indicators to signal forward progress, demystifying next steps without panic-inducing alert fatigue.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional authority with accessible civic warmth, utilizing deep constitutional blues, regenerative teal accents, energetic terracotta ochre, and calm safety emeralds.

### Color Tokens & Roles
- **Primary (`#1B365D` — Deep Navy):** Represents foundational law, authority, and judicial trust. Used for dominant headings, top navigation anchors, primary action buttons, and active status states.
- **Secondary (`#0E8388` — Deep Civic Teal):** Evokes clarity, calm guidance, and open communication. Used for progressive interactive elements, informational accents, active filters, and secondary buttons.
- **Tertiary (`#E06D14` — Saffron Terracotta):** Warm civic ochre used sparingly for crucial highlights, callout markers, step-completion pips, and urgent legal advisory banners.
- **Safety Emerald (`#1B8754`):** Functional semantic color indicating secured rights, positive confirmation, active helpline status, and verified legal precedent.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9FAF7`):** Warm off-white surface that mitigates harsh glare, creating a soft, paper-like civic publishing experience.
- **Neutral Text Base (`#1C2430`):** High-contrast slate charcoal preventing the eye fatigue caused by pure `#000000` on bright surfaces while preserving peak readability.
- **Border / Divider Tonal Neutral (`#E2E8F0`):** Subtle structural boundaries that establish form fields and card containers without visual noise.

## Typography

The type hierarchy prioritizes high information velocity and zero reading ambiguity. Built around `Inter` for its tall x-height, neutral aperture, open counters, and exceptional multi-lingual transliteration harmony across Indian digital environments.

### Typographic Rhythm & Execution
- **Strict Leading Ratios:** Body line heights maintain a minimum 1.5x proportion (`16px/24px` and `18px/28px`), preventing line-skipping for users with low vision or reading fatigue under duress.
- **Semantic Differentiation:** Section numbers, IPC/BNS statutory citations, and legal rights tags always adopt `label-md` or `label-lg` with uppercase tracking (`+0.02em`) to cleanly separate metadata from standard reading text.
- **Editorial Legibility:** For long-form awareness primers, line length is hard-clamped between `58` and `72` characters per row to ensure optimal reading ergonomics.

## Layout & Spacing

A modular 8-point baseline grid enforces predictable structural harmony throughout the application.

### Layout Blueprint
- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max Width 1280px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer page `margin: 2rem`. Content splits cleanly into a 4-column procedural index / breadcrumb sidebar and an 8-column reading / step-action workspace.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `gutter: 1.25rem` and `margin: 1.5rem`. Secondary information sidebars collapse into persistent bottom drawers or accordion summaries.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, <768px):** 4-column layout with `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and `margin-mobile: 1rem`. All instructional forms reflow into single-column vertical sequences with sticky bottom action controls.

### Density & Rhythms
- Card and container padding strictly scales from `space-md` (mobile) to `space-lg` (desktop).
- Visual dividers between unrelated law modules use `space-xl` to maintain clean conceptual boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system eschews floating, glossy, or distracting skeuomorphic shadows in favor of **Crisp Architectural Elevation** coupled with **Subtle Tonal Boundaries**. This reinforces grounding, authenticity, and clear tactile hierarchy.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F9FAF7`. The grounded background layer.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, List Items):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F9FAF7`, outlined with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border and an ambient, whisper-light drop shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Triggers, Sticky Step Trackers):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with an elevated border `1px solid #CBD5E1` and focused directional shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Emergency Hotlines, Safety Sheets, Modals):** Pure `#FFFFFF` overlay with a high-contrast backing backdrop scrim (`rgba(28, 36, 48, 0.6)`) and soft deep shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.16)`.
- **Emergency Action Bar:** Anchored to viewport bottom with a vibrant hairline top border (`2px solid #E06D14`) and shadow `0 -4px 16px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface implements **Level 2 Roundedness** (0.5rem base radius), projecting a warm, welcoming, and approachable character while avoiding juvenile or excessively playful curves.

### Shape Application Guidelines
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Primary Buttons, Action Chips):** Built with `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) corners to provide a friendly yet dependable ergonomic feel.
- **Containers & Structural Cards:** Use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to soften legal guidance cards, scenario checklists, and advice modules.
- **Modal Sheets & Drawer Surfaces:** Utilize `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) top edges on mobile screens to mimic natural unfolding sheets.
- **Badges, Law Citations, and Status Tags:** Use fully pill-shaped geometries (`9999px`) to create clear visual contrast against rectangular procedural cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Button (Core Action):** Solid `#1B365D` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded` (`8px`) border radius, height `48px`, font `label-lg`. Hover: `#12243E`. Focus: 3px ring of `#0E8388` at 40% opacity.
- **Secondary Button (Navigation/Explore):** `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px solid #0E8388` border, `#0E8388` text. Hover: Tinted `#F0FDF4`.
- **Emergency Call Button (High-Stakes):** `#E06D14` background with bold `#FFFFFF` text and white telephone/shield icon, providing an immediate tactile call-to-action.

### Step-by-Step Guidance Indicators
- **Procedural Steps (e.g., How to File an FIR):** Arranged vertically on mobile and horizontally on desktop. 
- **Step Node:** A `32px` circular indicator.
  - *Completed:* `#1B8754` background with a white checkmark icon.
  - *Active:* `#1B365D` background with white step number and an outer `4px` pulse halo (`#0E8388` at 20%).
  - *Upcoming:* Transparent background with `2px solid #CBD5E1` and `#64748B` numerical text.
- **Connecting Rail:** A continuous `2px` solid stroke (`#1B8754` when resolved, `#E2E8F0` when pending).

### Cards & Legal Scenario Modules
- **Law Breakdown Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `16px` padding, `rounded-lg` (`16px`). 
- **Accent Indicator Bar:** A vertical `4px` left border indicating category (e.g., `#0E8388` for Women's Safety, `#E06D14` for Consumer Rights, `#1B365D` for Fundamental Freedoms).
- **Header:** Features a top row with a category pill badge on the left and an estimated read time or jurisdiction indicator on the right.

### Chips & Statutary Badges
- **Status Pills:** Height `26px`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, `rounded-full`.
  - *Legal Right Badge:* Pale teal fill (`#E6F4F1`), deep teal text (`#0E8388`).
  - *Statute Citation (e.g., BNS Sec. 69):* Pale blue fill (`#EBF1F8`), deep navy text (`#1B365D`).
  - *Urgent Alert:* Pale terracotta fill (`#FDF1E8`), rich saffron text (`#B85207`).

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, text `body-md` (`#1C2430`), placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focus state shifts border directly to `#0E8388` with zero lag.
- **Safety Checklists & Rights Radios:** Custom `20px` checkboxes with `rounded` corners (`4px`). Unchecked: `1.5px solid #94A3B8`. Checked: `#1B8754` background containing a sharp white checkmark. Selection highlights the entire container with a 1px border of `#0E8388`.

### Specialized Civic Components
- **Plain-Language Legal Glossary Drawer:** Floating trigger opening a lightweight bottom card explaining legal terms (e.g., "Cognizable Offence", "Bail") in straightforward everyday vernacular.
- **Emergency Quick-Exit Trigger:** High-contrast fixed anchor in header allowing immediate redirection to a neutral search engine for user privacy and personal safety.
