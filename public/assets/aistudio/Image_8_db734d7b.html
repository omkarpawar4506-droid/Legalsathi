Here are the immediate steps you should take under Indian banking regulations & national cyber safety procedures:

1 Act Quickly (Golden Window)
Urgent

Dial the National Cyber Financial Helpline 1930 immediately. If reported early, the portal directly alerts the beneficiary bank's nodal officer to freeze the fraudulent recipient account or wallet before funds are withdrawn.

2 Lodge Bank Grievance (Within 3 Days)

File a written fraud report with your remitting bank & UPI provider (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). Under RBI Circular on Limiting Customer Liability, timely reporting minimizes or eliminates customer liability. Keep your 12-digit UPI UTR number saved.

3 File Formal Incident Acknowledgement

Register a formal complaint at the official portal cybercrime.gov.in. Download the generated acknowledgment receipt for local cyber police documentation.
